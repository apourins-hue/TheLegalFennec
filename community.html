Send us your work.

The Legal Fennec welcomes original perspectives from jurists, students and beyond. If you have an idea about law, technology or society, we would like to hear about it.

What you can propose

Essays, explainers, case notes, thoughtful criticism, comparative legal analysis and practical resources. We especially welcome emerging voices and multidisciplinary work. Publication is selective and subject to editorial review.

Before you submit

Tell us what question your piece addresses, why it matters and which sources support it. Please disclose substantial AI assistance and any conflicts of interest. Do not send confidential legal documents, personal case records or material you lack permission to share.
