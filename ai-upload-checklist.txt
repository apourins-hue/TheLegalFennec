THE LEGAL FENNEC
Before uploading unpublished creative material to an AI service
Educational checklist — first edition, October 2026

[ ] Do I own the material, or have permission from all relevant rights holders to share it?
[ ] Is the screenplay, treatment or agreement confidential or subject to an NDA?
[ ] Does a contract with a client, producer or co-author restrict third-party disclosures?
[ ] What do the specific AI provider's current terms, privacy notices and settings say about retention, access and training?
[ ] Can I minimize the disclosure, remove personal data or use an approved confidential alternative?
[ ] Have I checked applicable privacy, copyright and professional confidentiality obligations in the relevant jurisdiction?
[ ] Should I obtain written approval from the relevant producer, employer or rights holder before proceeding?
[ ] Can I document the decision, settings used, consent and any necessary safeguards?

Important: This checklist is general educational information, not legal advice or a guarantee of confidentiality. It does not establish whether a particular AI service is safe, or whether any disclosure is lawful. Check up-to-date contractual terms and applicable law, and seek qualified counsel when needed.
