02 / Explore your fit

Could law & technology be for you?

Six questions to help you reflect on the work, not judge whether you are “good at law.” You do not need to be a lawyer or know how to code to be curious about technology regulation.

1. How do you feel about reading dense legal texts or judgments?
2. When an AI-related law or policy changes, what is your reaction?
3. How do you feel about questions with no obvious correct answer?
4. Which part of technology interests you most?
5. Would you enjoy researching and writing about one legal question for several weeks?
6. What are you hoping a master’s will help you do?
